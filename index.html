Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the PlaneSpotted app ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use it. PlaneSpotted is a logbook for planespotters. We built it to be as privacy-respecting as possible: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data to anyone.

Your Logbook

Your sightings, trips, photos, notes and tags are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is turned on (Settings → Sync with iCloud), they are also stored in your own private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. Only you can read your private iCloud data. We cannot see it.

What You Choose to Share

Sharing is optional and controlled by Share my stats in your profile. While it is on and you have set a display name, the App publishes the following to the App's public iCloud database, which other PlaneSpotted users can see:

  • Your profile: display name, home airport, camera, website, friend code and totals (sightings, airframes, aircraft types, airports, countries and trips), plus the spotters you have added as friends.
  • Airport summaries: for each airport where you have logged sightings, the registrations, aircraft types and airlines you logged there, how many times, and when you were last there. These appear on airport pages and in leaderboards.
  • Recent sightings: each sighting from the past year (registration, aircraft type, airline, airport and date), used for the weekly, monthly and yearly leaderboards.

Your photos and notes are never shared. Turning Share my stats off deletes all of the above from the public database.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account creation, sign-up or login. PlaneSpotted uses your existing iCloud account.
  • We do not collect your email address or phone number.
  • We do not collect your location. If you allow it, your location is used on your device only, to suggest the nearest airport.
  • We do not upload your photos. Photos you pick stay in your logbook, and images you save from the App are added to your photo library only when you tap Save.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising services.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, for any reason.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit) stores your logbook and anything you choose to share. Apple's privacy policy governs iCloud.
  • adsbdb.com receives the aircraft registrations you log, so the App can fill in the type, airline and a reference photo.
  • pics.avs.io supplies airline logos, using the airline's code.
  • Instagram receives an image only if you tap Share to Instagram Story.

None of these requests include your name or any other personal information.

Other Spotters, Blocking and Reporting

Display names you see on airport pages, in friend requests and in leaderboards are chosen by other users. You can block any spotter, which hides them from you, and report a name that is offensive, either from inside the App or with the form below. We review reports and remove offending names within 24 hours.

Children's Privacy

PlaneSpotted is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your logbook stays on your device and in your private iCloud until you delete it. Deleting the App, or turning off iCloud for PlaneSpotted in your device's Settings, removes it from that device.
  • Shared information stays in the public database while Share my stats is on. Sightings older than a year are removed automatically. Turning sharing off deletes everything you have shared.
  • To have anything else deleted, contact us below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
